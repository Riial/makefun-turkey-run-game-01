# FOWL PLAY — a turkey escape game

A side-view endless runner for the make.fun bounty
[turkey-run-game-01](https://make.fun/bounty/turkey-run-game-01): a turkey
sprints from the farmyard, through the barn, into the kitchen, dodging
fences, rolling pumpkins, falling carving knives and flaming ovens while the
farmer chases it down. Escape 2000 m to be pardoned.

## How to play

- **Jump** — Space / ↑ / W / click
- **Slide** — ↓ / S (slide under hanging knives, slip across puddles)
- **Dash** — Shift (uses the dash meter)
- **Hide** — ↓ next to a haystack, crate or pot stack (the farmer searches, then loses you)
- **Pause** — P / Esc · **Mute** — M · **Restart** — R

Serve locally with `node serve.ts` and open http://localhost:8080.
